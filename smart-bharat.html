Project Detail

Smart Bharat

An AI-powered civic companion built for Devengers PromptWars 2026 to help citizens access services, report issues, and get personalized assistance.

Overview

Smart Bharat is a full-stack civic-tech platform built solo within a fixed hackathon submission window. It helps citizens ask questions about government schemes, understand documents and processes in plain language, report civic issues, track complaint status, and explore a curated directory of government schemes with AI-summarized eligibility and benefits.

Technology Badges

Highlights

  • AI chatbot powered by Google Gemini for citizen queries
  • Bilingual English and Hindi support for wider accessibility
  • Complaint reporting for roads, water, electricity, and sanitation
  • Complaint tracking with live status updates
  • Government scheme directory with AI-summarized eligibility and benefits
  • Solo full-stack build covering system design, prompt engineering, and deployment
